Caveats and Excluded Rows

Known limitations and rows excluded from top-line totals.

  • Coverage is directional. ManyVids Jan/Feb top-line earnings are manually backfilled from the monthly sales table, but title-level detail for those months is unavailable.
  • OnlyFans is sourced from native OF statement PDFs when imported, otherwise Supercreator account exports or the labeled creators-table fallback. OF revenue sources are deduped by date so overlapping setup/backfill exports do not inflate totals. Native OF post and message statistics are supplemental content metrics and are excluded from top-line revenue totals.

  • Curvage net uses daily purchases through the latest daily export, then item-level earnings for newer dates.
